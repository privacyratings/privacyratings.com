# Code of Conduct

Privacy Ratings is a shared, public resource. Keep every issue, pull request and discussion useful and respectful.

- Argue with evidence, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and say when you have a connection to a service being discussed.
- No harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, spam or self-promotion.
- Do not post anyone's private information, including your own.

Maintainers may edit, hide or lock comments and block accounts that break these rules. Report problems by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).

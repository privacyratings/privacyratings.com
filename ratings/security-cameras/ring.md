The FTC charged Ring with letting employees and contractors access customers' private videos and with failing to protect accounts from hackers; the settlement order required Ring to pay $5.8 million in consumer refunds and to delete data products built from unlawfully accessed videos. See the [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/05/ftc-says-ring-employees-illegally-surveilled-customers-failed-stop-hackers-taking-control-users) and [case page](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/2023113-ring-llc).

Ring publishes [law enforcement guidelines and twice-yearly reports](https://ring.com/law-enforcement-information-requests) with the number of government demands it receives and how it responds.

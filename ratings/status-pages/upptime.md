Forward Email's status page, [status.forwardemail.net](https://status.forwardemail.net), runs on Upptime and loads no trackers. Documentation is at [upptime.js.org](https://upptime.js.org).

## Contributions from the Forward Email team

The Forward Email team, which maintains this site, contributes to Upptime under the GitHub account [titanism](https://github.com/titanism). Contributions include:

- [Light and dark themes](https://github.com/upptime/status-page/commit/a8a3782) that follow the visitor's system setting, and [fixes to initial page loading](https://github.com/upptime/status-page/commit/9104d06) in the status page.
- [Sending SMS alerts to multiple numbers](https://github.com/upptime/uptime-monitor/commit/7766780).
- [IPv6 monitoring](https://github.com/upptime/uptime-monitor/commit/fcebd9e) and a [fix to IPv6 checks](https://github.com/upptime/uptime-monitor/commit/3cc3be2).
- An [SSL certificate expiry check](https://github.com/upptime/uptime-monitor/commit/1793600).
- Core reliability fixes: [TCP ping false positives](https://github.com/upptime/uptime-monitor/commit/a5d7ec8) and [retries on failed HTTP checks](https://github.com/upptime/uptime-monitor/commit/35da82a).

See all [uptime-monitor](https://github.com/upptime/uptime-monitor/commits?author=titanism) and [status-page](https://github.com/upptime/status-page/commits?author=titanism) commits.
